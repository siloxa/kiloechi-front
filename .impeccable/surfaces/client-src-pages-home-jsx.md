# Surface brief: Kiloechi site (home, menu, cart, checkout, account) + admin panel

Scope: whole customer site (Persuade: home, menu; Operate: cart, checkout, account, auth) and the admin panel (Operate). Audience: Iranian customers ordering fried chicken on phones; restaurant staff running orders. Action: add dishes and order; staff move orders through statuses. Constraints: Persian RTL, Toman, red + yellow are the only kept brand traits, real food photos and a logo will replace placeholders, nothing invented (no fake reviews, numbers, or claims).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ordering at Kiloechi works like the counter: you take a slip, your number lights up on the red call board, and it is called hot. Refuses the delivery-app default of a split photo hero over a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Brand red field (#d40f14) carrying most of the page; a near-black call-board housing (#1a0708) with yellow LED readouts (#ffc400); cool white thermal slips (#fdfdfb) with torn sawtooth edges, dashed perforation rules and black thermal ink; red rubber stamps that appear only on state change (added, discounted, ready). Lalezar display, Vazirmatn text, dot-matrix LED numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees the board, reads that ordering is a slip away, adds a dish straight from today's slip, watches it print onto their own slip, checks out, then follows their own number lighting up lamp by lamp on the board.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edge-to-edge call board across the top (logo, LED message with true data, nav, cart count in LED). Below on red: a huge yellow Lalezar headline and one line of copy at the inline-start; a large framed food photo; at the inline-end, "today's slip" hanging from the board's printer slot with featured dishes, prices and working add buttons. Primary action: add on the slip, then "your slip" checkout.

FORM: The Counter Ticket (فیش و شماره), candidate 4 of 7 on my resonance list. seed:a9a174323fd12b06. Raises kept: one repeated slip row as the ruler, stamp red only on state change (VU meter); marks on the price itself (zine); labeled status lamps (silk canopy); board as the horizon of every page (indoor sun); the added dish lifts forward (console); selected states change pattern, not tint (Memphis). Signature interaction: adding a dish prints a new line into your slip with a paper-feed motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
